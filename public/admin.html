Group your gifts under headings on your shared page, like “Books”, “Kitchen” or “Board games”. New gifts are put in the section that best matches their name, and you can always change it. Gifts without a section are shown last, under “Other ideas”.

    Add from any store

    Some stores (like eBay and Amazon) block “Fetch details”. This bookmark works around that: it reads the product page you're already looking at and opens this page with the details filled in.

    ➕ Add to gift list
    1. Drag the Add to gift list button to your browser's bookmarks bar. (If dragging doesn't work, as in Safari, bookmark any page, then edit the bookmark and paste the copied code as its address.)
    2. On a product page, click the bookmark. This page opens with the form filled in. Review it and save.
    3. If this site's address changes (for example, a new custom domain), add the bookmark again from the new address.

    Gifts people are getting

    To keep things a surprise, you can't see which gifts people have said they'll get. After a birthday or the holidays, you can clear them all so your list starts fresh. (Delete or hide gifts you received first.)
